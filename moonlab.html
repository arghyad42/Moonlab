Welcome to the Moon Lab

We investigate the coordination of the cell cycle, cellular growth, and human disease models. Using Drosophila melanogaster as our primary model organism, we combine molecular biology, genetics, and advanced bioimaging to uncover the fundamental mechanisms of development and disease.

Research Areas

🧬

Cell Cycle & E2F Genetics

Investigating the in vivo function of E2F transcription factors and the Retinoblastoma (RB) homologue, understanding their role in coordinating endoreplication and cellular proliferation.

🔬

ER Stress & Organelle Dynamics

Uncovering the molecular consequences of PERK and de2f1b mutations on endoplasmic reticulum homeostasis, cytoplasmic DNA accumulation, and cellular stress responses in salivary glands.

💻

Quantitative Bioimaging

Utilizing high-resolution confocal microscopy, automated Fiji (ImageJ) segmentation algorithms, and R-based genomic alignment to extract precise quantitative data from complex biological samples.
